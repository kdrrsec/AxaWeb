Case

Bandendepot - online warehouse for tyres

Bandendepot positions itself as an online warehouse for quality tyres. The website combines a clear promise, a direct route from interest to enquiry, and an accessible quote form for the Netherlands and Belgium.

Category Business website

Live bandendepot.nl

  • Website
  • Conversion-focused design
  • Quote form

Client

About the client

Bandendepot serves individuals and businesses looking for tyres for passenger cars and commercial vehicles. Fast delivery, sharp pricing, and advice are central to the site, with a phone number for direct contact.

Context

Challenge

Visitors need to understand quickly what Bandendepot offers and how to take the next step - without a heavy catalogue flow. The site must build trust in a price-sensitive market and keep the path to a quote or telephone advice as short as possible.

Approach

Our approach

We built a clear, conversion-focused landing structure: a strong hero with positioning, straightforward navigation to assortment and service, and a prominent quote form with relevant fields such as licence plate and tyre size.

  • Clear hierarchy from promise to action
  • Form tailored to tyre advice
  • Direct call CTA alongside online requests
  • Calm, readable sections without clutter

Design

Design

The design is light and commercial: white as the base, Inter as the typeface, and a yellow accent for primary actions. Large typography and uppercase navigation give the site a no-nonsense business feel that fits a product with a practical purchase moment.

Development

Development

The website runs on WordPress and is structured around a clear content flow: hero, explanation, quote form, and trust arguments. Form fields support a concrete request (including licence plate, car brand, and tyre size).

Responsive

Responsive optimisation

On mobile, the core flow stays intact: the promise, primary CTAs, and quote form remain reachable without horizontal scroll. Navigation and buttons are adapted for touch, so visitors can get in touch quickly on the go.

Technology

Technical highlights

What is technically visible and verifiable on the live website:

  • WordPress as the content platform
  • Viewport meta and mobile-friendly layout
  • Structured quote form with privacy reference
  • Clear internal navigation to assortment, service, and about us

Hosting

Hosting and maintenance

The live environment runs on WordPress. Content, form handling, and privacy references are part of the online presence; further hosting arrangements are set per engagement.

Result

Result

The result is a complete, easy-to-understand website where Bandendepot positions itself as an online tyre partner. Visitors can explore the offer, call directly, or request a quote through a focused form.
